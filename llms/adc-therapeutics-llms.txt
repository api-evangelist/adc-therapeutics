# ADC Therapeutics

> ADC Therapeutics SA (NYSE: ADCT) is a commercial-stage biotechnology company headquartered at the Biopôle in Lausanne, Switzerland, with US operations in New Jersey. It discovers and develops antibody drug conjugates (ADCs) for hematologic malignancies and solid tumors. Its approved product ZYNLONTA (loncastuximab tesirine-lpyl) is a CD19-directed ADC for relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma.
